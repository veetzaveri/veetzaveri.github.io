Medical AI · Clinical Waveforms

Non-invasive Intracranial Pressure Waveform Reconstruction with Deep Learning

LSTM / GRU V-Net TCN Transformer Multimodal Learning

Co-first-authored work on reconstructing continuous intracranial-pressure waveforms from routinely acquired physiological signals. I share responsibility for methodology, software, analysis, data curation, visualization, and manuscript preparation.

  • Studied 158 adult patients and approximately 5,322 hours of waveform data.
  • Developed and evaluated LSTM, GRU, multiscale V-Net, TCN, and Transformer-based models using ABP, PPG, and ECG.
  • Evaluated early- and late-fusion strategies and waveform-aware training objectives.
  • Conducted independent cross-institutional validation from a 47-patient internal cohort to a 111-patient external cohort.
  • Built the physiological signal-processing pipeline to synchronize concurrent waveforms, construct 30-second prediction windows, and resample high-frequency signals to 125 Hz.
  • Developed automated signal-quality filtering for flatline, noisy, periodic, and physiologically implausible waveform segments.
External-validation performance: Across five architectures, MAE ranged from 4.276–4.946 mmHg and Pearson correlation from 0.599–0.722.

Topology · Robotics

A Motion Planning Algorithm in a Figure Eight Track

Topology Motion Planning Algorithms

Equal-contribution research applying configuration-space topology to collision-free coordination of two robots moving on a figure-eight track.

  • Proved that the motion-planning problem has topological complexity 3.
  • Constructed an explicit algorithm with three continuous motion-planning instructions.
  • Used configuration-space topology to characterize instabilities in robotic motion-planning algorithms.
  • Published in the PUMP Journal of Undergraduate Research in 2023.
