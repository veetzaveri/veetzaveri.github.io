Medical AI

Non-invasive intracranial pressure waveform estimation

GRU Encoder–Decoder Multimodal External Validation

This collaborative project studies whether continuous intracranial pressure waveforms can be estimated from routinely measured physiological signals. The work combines signal processing, deep learning, and cross-site validation.

  • Developed and evaluated recurrent and multiscale encoder–decoder models.
  • Studied ECG-, PPG-, and multimodal signal configurations.
  • Used patient-level evaluation to reduce leakage between training and validation data.
  • Evaluated model generalization across independent clinical datasets without fine-tuning.
  • Explored waveform-aware objectives and morphology-sensitive evaluation.

Published research examining adversarial robustness in remote-sensing imagery. The project developed my interest in model reliability, failure modes, and rigorous evaluation under intentional perturbations.

Optimization

Domain-aware model selection and optimization

Ongoing empirical work exploring how optimization and hyperparameter selection can account for performance variation across multiple source domains rather than optimizing only a pooled average.

  • Compare source-domain-aware objective functions for model selection.
  • Study whether source-domain performance statistics predict unseen-domain robustness.
  • Evaluate optimization methods under distribution shift.
